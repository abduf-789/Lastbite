FAQ

Good to know

Is Preventing Sell food safe?

Every listing shows preparation time, storage condition, and a safe-consumption window. Listings automatically stop being available once that window passes.

Can I both sell and order?

Yes — one LastBite account can hold both Consumer and Staff access. Switch modes any time from the top of the page.

How is the rescue price decided?

A transparent pricing engine applies a 60–70% discount based on time remaining, quantity, and category — sellers can't bypass it without admin approval.

Where do donations go?

To verified nearby children's homes and community kitchens that have confirmed they're currently accepting food.
